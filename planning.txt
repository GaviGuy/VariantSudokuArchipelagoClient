YAML settings and generation:
    End goal is to solve the boss puzzle

    How many puzzles should there be? (excluding pillars and the boss)
    (default 10)

    How hard should the boss be?
    (default 3)

    How to unlock the boss?
    * found randomly, just like any other puzzle
    * (default) unlocked by solving n other puzzles
    * unlocked by solving all n pillar puzzles

    if n puzzle unlock, how many puzzles to unlock? expressed as a percentage of non-boss puzzles
    (default 50)

    if n pillar puzzles,
        how hard should they be, in stars
        (default 2)

        how many should there be
        (note: pillars exist ON TOP OF the base number of puzzles)
        (default 4)
    
    for all other puzzles,
        minimum diff
        (default 1)

        maximum diff
        (default 1)

        diff distribution
        * random - tier distribution is left up to chance         eg. ⡆⡀⡄⡇⡀
        * flat - all allowed ratings show up roughly equally      eg. ⡄⡄⡄⡄⡄
        * bell - most puzzles near the center of the range        eg. ⡀⡄⡇⡄⡀
        * exponential - each tier is roughly 50% of the previous  eg. ⡇⡄⡀⡀⡀

        minimum length
        (default none)
        
        maximum length
        (default 1 hour, however that's expressed)

    for all filler items
        ratio of puzzle auto-solves
        (default 1)

        ratio of hints (assuming I have a hint system)
        (default 2)

        ratio of puzzle rerolls (will roll from an easier pool)
        (default 2)

    excluded tags
    (default none)
